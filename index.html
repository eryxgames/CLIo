CLIo

Games in your terminal - and here, in the browser.

All games (CLIo's menu) The tech demo Make a game: the editor

Python and the games are fetched the first time (about 12 MB) and run in this browser; saves stay in it. On a phone, tap the picture: its sides turn, the top steps, the centre fires, the bottom uses; swipe to walk. Keys are under the picture too (abc opens the keyboard). For sound, press a key or tap the picture once. The browser draws the pictures several times slower than a terminal, and a phone slower again. CLIo itself runs in a real terminal, much faster and with every option: github.com/eryxgames/CLIo.
